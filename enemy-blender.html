Finished models — your ten picks

Clay spheres are gone. These are CC0 meshes from Quaternius, lit and snapped to transparent PNGs the same way as the tree. They are low-poly and stylized — faces, weapons, wings — not Jackson’s Moria. Each card names the actual mesh so you can keep, swap, or dump the pack.
