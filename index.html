Tower Defense

Six towers. Ten maps. Build beside the path — never on it — and hold the line.

How to play

  • Tower dock sits under the map — Arrow, Cannon, Ice, Lightning, Fire, Poison.
  • Pick a tower, then click green grass beside the brown path.
  • Trees are decoration — not build pads. Path tiles are blocked.
  • Click enemies or towers anytime (even paused) to inspect them.
  • Pause freezes combat but leaves the map visible so you can plan.

Select level

Levels unlock in order. Difficulty climbs every stage.
