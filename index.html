Tower Defense

Eight towers with skill paths, three barracks, flyers, ten biomes. Build beside the path — never on it — and hold the line.

How to play

  • Two-row build bar — towers on top, Warrior / Knight / Paladin barracks below. Tap an icon for full stats, tap again to cancel.
  • Pick a building, then click open grass beside the path.
  • Skill paths — upgrade towers or barracks to level 3, then choose one of two specializations (permanent).
  • Barracks — deploy up to three troops. Set Flag for their rally. Troops block ground enemies only — flyers need anti-air towers.
  • Flyers — Wisps and Gargoyles. Cannon and barracks cannot hit them; Arrow, elementals, Radiant, and Void can.
  • Pause freezes combat but you can still place, upgrade, sell, and set flags. Use First / Strong / Close targeting on selected towers.
  • Blocked tiles — path, water, trees, and rocks. Later levels steal more of the best pads on purpose.
  • Mythical foes — orcs, ghouls, trolls, ghosts, bone serpents, liches, and more.
  • Click enemies or towers anytime (even paused) to inspect them.
  • Mute toggles procedural sound effects.
