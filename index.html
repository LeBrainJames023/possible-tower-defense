Tower Defense

Six towers. Five worlds. Fifty maps. Build beside the path — never on it.

How to play

  • Click grass beside the path — a 2×3 tray opens next to that tile. Pick a tower to see range, attack, and fire time, then Build.
  • Five worlds — Forest, then Desert, Ice, Fire, The Hollow. Each land has two local foes, a wave-7 champion, and its own wave-10 boss. You can open a locked land to see its maps; playing it waits until you beat the previous world.
  • Click your towers or enemies to inspect, upgrade, or sell — the map stays on screen.
  • Flyers stay off the ground. Cannon cannot hit them — Arrow and Lightning can.
